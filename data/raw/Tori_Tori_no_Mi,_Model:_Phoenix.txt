The Tori Tori no Mi, Model: Phoenix is a Mythical Zoan -type Devil Fruit that grants the power to transform into a phoenix and half-phoenix hybrid at will, as well as self-regenerate (and heal others) using blue phoenix flames. The fruit was eaten by Polo Marco .

The Tori Tori no Mi, Model: Phoenix resembles a pineapple : an oval fruit with green skin segmented into round fruitlets, which each feature a signature Devil Fruit swirl in the middle and come to a prickly point on top. Instead of the T-shaped stem typically seen with Devil Fruits, this fruit possesses a crown of long, blade-shaped leaves that stiffly point up, form the occasional Devil Fruit swirl, and overall resemble the blue-yellow phoenix flames that this fruit grants access to.

The ability user can transform into a mythical phoenix: a large, vaguely peafowl -shaped bird that is completely shrouded in blue (partially yellow) flames. This firebird form comes with two sizeable wings featuring eyespot feathers, a long and slender neck, a yellow-flamed head crest, legs with sharp talons, and a trio of long, yellow tail feathers made of closed, bound-together circles. Notably through Marco's use, no full-bodied Human-Beast Form has been seen (that evenly spreads over his entire body), only partial morphing of his arms and legs into wings and bird legs respectively; whether or not this partial transformation is in fact this Zoan's default Human-Beast Form remains unconfirmed.

As with all Zoans, the ability user's transformations greatly bolster their physical abilities, granting (in Marco's case) strength high enough to hold back two powerful Ancient Zoan users simultaneously. As a bird-type Zoan , it bestows the power of flight in both the Human-Beast and Beast Form, allowing the user to excel in matters of combat mobility and travel. Either one of the user's phoenix forms also comes with sharp sets of talons on the feet that can deliver slashing damage via kicks.

The special "mythical" trait this Zoan confers upon the user is the ability to summon the blue flames of the phoenix—dubbed the "Blue Flames of Resurrection" ( 復活の青い炎 , Fukkatsu no Aoi Honō , VIZ: Blue Flame of Revival) and "Flames of Regeneration" ( 再生の炎 , Saisei no Honō , Funimation: Flames of Restoration) . The user is automatically clad in these flames upon transforming, but doing so is not strictly necessary for the flames to be conjured, at least in smaller amounts. Notably, while the phoenix flames do not burn, spread, or exude heat the way mundane fire does, their "special" nature makes them comparatively stronger; during a clash between Marco and Big Mom , Marco was able to blow away and cause pain to Prometheus , a living fire homie born from Big Mom's super-strong soul .

Rather than burn, the phoenix flames have extraordinary healing power. When cloaked in them, the user can rapidly regenerate all injuries inflicted onto them—even mortal ones—much like a phoenix rising anew from its ashes. This makes the user enormously resilient and able to undergo extreme punishment without feeling pain or being slowed down. In Marco's case, devastating injuries like being riddled with bullets, having his head and torso run through with blades, chunks of his head and neck being blown off, or one of his wings being severed all have elicited little reaction from him, the flames erasing the damage about as quickly as it occurs. The ability user can enter their phoenix form to automatically heal all prior-accumulated damage in one go, or else heal by summoning the flames in isolation, without having to transform. In full phoenix mode, non- Haki attacks may simply pass through the user's body as though it were pure flame, in a way superficially resembling a Logia 's invulnerability; the defining difference is that the user merely regenerates and retains a tangible, solid body beneath the fire. Thus, even injuries sustained under Seastone contact can be healed by the flames once contact with said material stops.

In battle, the phoenix flames provide exceptional defense, their constant healing effect allowing the user to ward off extremely powerful attacks by using their own body—notably their large wings—as a shield. In doing so, Marco has successfully blocked and stood firm against overwhelming assaults like Marine Admiral Kizaru 's volley of light bullets , Admiral Akainu 's scorching magma fist , or the Emperor Kaidou 's castle-leveling breath of dragon fire . Moreover, while the blue flames lack the raw destructiveness of natural fire, they can still be wielded offensively, as Marco demonstrated by using fire-based attacks that, instead of burning, create powerful shockwaves and/or deliver blunt-force strikes that send opponents flying. Furthermore, any part of the phoenix body that is separated from the main body, like a wing, remains under the user's control, allowing it to be reshaped and used in a counterattack.

Beyond self-recovery, the phoenix flames can be applied to treat others, even large groups of people if the user manually spreads out their fire in a large wave. When cast over someone else's injuries, the flames speed up the natural healing process to promote faster recovery, e.g. fixing a broken limb so that it is fully functional the next day. Once the flames have lit other people, they stay with and continuously heal them, the recipients' stamina permitting. This may even extend to diseases, as shown when Marco employed his fire as a temporary countermeasure against Queen the Plague 's cold-inducing virus , raising the body heat of all infected parties caught by his flames to halt the virus' spread and progression. That said, the ability user cannot mend others as well as they can their own body, the healing having a much smaller and slower effect on others.

A downside of the phoenix flames is that, being purely for healing, they have none of the inherent attack potency (heat and ability to burn) of regular fire. However, they can still be used offensively to some degree and, owing to their "special" nature, can be used neutralize normal fire.

While the user's regeneration can nullify most attacks, it has a limit. That is, the user is not truly immortal, suggesting that damage beyond a certain threshold (sustained all at once, over time, or both) might overwhelm the healing factor. Regardless, Haki is established as a reliable counter to the regeneration: while attempts at healing or defense are still possible, the damage from Haki-infused attacks persists. This was first demonstrated at Marineford where, despite ignoring hits from gunfire, a flying Marco was easily punched out of the air by Vice Admiral Garp , presumably using Haki. During the Summit War and later the Raid on Onigashima , despite Marco instantly healing fatal wounds upon receiving them, he still accrued some minor damage over time, like scrapes and bruises, especially after facing more powerful foes; in the aftermath of either conflict, Marco was seen in bandages, hinting at injuries that he could or chose not to heal. Related to this, the regeneration may be slower if the user is caught off-guard or not transformed, as seen when Marco was pierced by Kizaru's lasers in his Human Form but did not instantly heal, despite the wounds' severity. Lastly, while the user can shrug off damage from attacks, they remain susceptible to restraint, e.g. being grabbed and held in place.

The phoenix flames are known to draw upon the user's stamina, making prolonged use of them physically taxing until the flames can no longer be effectively created. On Onigashima , after fighting two powerful opponents for an extended period and having previously used his flames on dozens of other people, Marco was temporarily put out of commission, which onlookers attributed to his stamina being depleted. This also holds true for when the phoenix flames are used on others, being not as strong in their healing as when applied to the user's own body and fading once the recipient's stamina has run out. The flames' limited effect on others is also evident from how Marco was unable to help his elderly captain Whitebeard , who normally had to be hooked up to medical machinery as a result of his declining health.

Otherwise, the user suffers from the standard weaknesses of any Devil Fruit user, like Seastone fully sealing their powers upon contact.

Marco's abilities are well-known and have garnered him the epithet Marco the Phoenix . He extensively relies on his transformation for aerial combat, and thanks to its physical and "mythical" qualities, his powers are highly versatile, allowing him to excel in offense, defense, and support roles during battle. Marco's bird physiology allow him to fly at high speeds and utilize his human legs to deliver strong Haki-imbued kicks or knee strikes, and his phoenix legs to land slashing talon strikes. Marco can further employ his wings as a means of restraining foes, even extremely strong and fully transformed Ancient Zoan users, as seen when Marco easily subdued King and Queen —two of the strongest members of the Beasts Pirates with one billion-plus bounties —at once. For direct offense, Marco can shape his flames into shockwave-like blasts that, rather than burn opponents, deal concussive damage to or send them flying.

In combat, Marco's regeneration serves a passive, defensive purpose, enabling him to outright ignore most physical attacks he is faced with. Ranged and melee blows, even weapons, pass right through him while any wounds received heal near-instantly, closing up without leaving a scar; this may make it seem even to formidable adversaries that Marco is invincible. Among other things, he has been observed shielding his allies using his own body and flames before simply healing himself up afterwards.

In terms of healing others, Marco's flames have shown immense usefulness on battlefields with a lot of potential allies to be mended, whether from injury or infection. For years, Marco served as an efficient ship doctor for the Whitebeard Pirates , and following the crew's disbandment, he went on to do doctoring on Sphinx with his powers.

Marco has made practical use of flying to traverse battlefields quickly and bypass enemies on the ground, in order to supervise whatever fighting is going on (as he did during the Summit War of Marineford in Whitebeard 's stead), to relay important intel, and to carry others with him. In his apprentice days, Marco acted as a scout for the Whitebeard Pirates, investigating unknown islands.

Marco can seamlessly switch between his Beast or Human-Beast Forms. Unlike most Zoan users, in his half-phoenix form, he undergoes a more selective transformation, usually shifting just his arms into phoenix wings and his legs into phoenix legs, while his head and torso remain unchanged. If this is a mark of Marco's superior control over his powers or the Human-Beast Form's default look is unclear.

Three decades ago, a 15-year-old Marco had already eaten the fruit and seemed well-versed in utilizing his phoenix transformation. Using its power, he became reputed as Marco the Phoenix.
