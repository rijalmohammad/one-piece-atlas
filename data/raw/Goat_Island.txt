Goat Island is an island in the East Blue that the Alvida Pirates used as a hideout. It is where Luffy first met Koby and Alvida .

It is a small, rocky island where small rocks covered the shoreline and a small forest is visible.

At some point, the Alvida Pirates made this island their base. After making Koby their chore boy, he planned for years to escape, building a small boat near the forest.

Once Luffy defeated Alvida, Koby was freed, and the Alvida Pirates later merged with the Buggy Pirates . The bases on the island are presumably abandoned since the crew is currently in the Grand Line .

The anime adaptation of the Romance Dawn Arc cut out Goat Island, moving Luffy's meeting with Koby to the Lady Mary instead.

However, in later flashbacks to the scene in the Episode of Luffy special and Episode 1153 , the island makes an appearance. Although the special still has them meeting on a ship, on Alvida's Miss Love Duck , with the island only appearing in the background; while episode 1153 has the two actually meet on Goat Island, like how it was in the manga.
