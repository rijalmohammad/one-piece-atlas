Enies Lobby was a World Government establishment located on a Paradise island where night never fell. It, along with Impel Down and Marineford , comprised the three major Government strongholds triangulating the Tarai Current via the Gates of Justice .

Reputed worldwide as the Judicial Island ( 司法の島 , Shihō no Shima ) , Enies Lobby hosted both CP9 headquarters and the (mostly ceremonial) courthouse for processing Impel Down sentences. These were attended by a mixed garrison of Cipher Pol agents, Marines , and special personnel, totaling some 10,000 troops in all.

After a combined attack from Straw Hat Pirates , the Galley-La Company and the Franky Family —which inadvertently triggered a full-scale Buster Call —the island was completely annihilated, and left in ruins. It is unknown if anything has been rebuilt since.

It was the main setting of the Enies Lobby Arc .

CP9 is the most secretive of the Cipher Pol units; only the government and high-level Marines are supposed to know about their existence. They are all masters of the Six Powers , a style of martial art which requires years of physical training to be able to use. Their headquarters is located in Enies Lobby.

The architecture of Enies Lobby is like no other and stands out. This is normal for islands in the One Piece world, especially in the Grand Line .

The architecture of the town in the center part of the island, in the center of the black void and the waterfall, contains brown buildings that appear to be made of clay or another such similar material. Each one appears to be unique, and some are taller than others.

CP9 Headquarters and Judicial Buildings

Each of these buildings has the most well-known architecture in Enies Lobby. They are large white marble buildings, with what seem to be jade green squares on the sides, surrounded by thin gold lines. The green is also on the top of some of the buildings, such as the courthouse.

Enies Lobby's environment is highly unusual even by Grand Line standards: known as the "afternoon island" ( 昼島 , hiru-jima ) and the "never-night island" ( 不夜島 , fuya-jima ) , it apparently always stands in full daylight, even when large storms such as the Aqua Laguna are raging on nearby waters. There are also many whirlpools behind the island and a giant gate, the Gates of Justice, which only ever open a little bit to let criminals through and, when opened, stop the whirlpools because of the tides.

Possibly the strangest thing about Enies Lobby is the large waterfall under the island, leading to nowhere except a huge black void, although there is water down there. The waterfall is a natural anomaly that does not seem to exist anywhere else. With the exception of a single bridge of land connecting the island to the sea, the island is basically floating above a hole in the ocean. It was never explained how this hole in the ocean is made possible, where the water goes, or how the island is able to maintain its structural integrity.

Enies Lobby has a large courthouse on the southeast corner of the island, whose judge is the three-headed giant named Baskerville . Though they are actually three different people who like to jointly wear a single outfit out of their friendship for each other. They each call themselves a different name; the one on the left, Bas, is known as Left Baskerville, who believes everyone is automatically guilty and should be locked up. The one on the right, Kerville, is known as Right Baskerville, who believes that all criminals are actually innocent and should be let go. The one in the middle usually calls himself "Princess" but picks different names occasionally. He claims to be neutral about a criminal's guilt, but he actually proposes extremes that go beyond either of the other two, such as a death sentence. This causes the Left and Right to bonk him on the head and remind him he is supposed to be the perfectly fair one.

Additionally, the jury consists of criminals that have already been given sentences. No one can be tried and be found not guilty because the jurors only want to bring as many people as possible to Impel Down with them to suffer the same fate as they have. Tom was the only one known to have been able to come close to avoiding this because of his work on the Sea Train . However, due to Spandam 's meddling, Tom was executed.

Before the revelation of the rigged jury, the rumor was that the courthouse was empty and that people who were taken to Enies Lobby were automatically found guilty without a trial.

The back of the Courthouse is equipped with a drawbridge that connects the main island with the Tower of Justice. The controls for the bridge are located in two towers on the sides of the courthouse.

The CP9's headquarters, the Tower of Justice ( 司法の塔 , Shihō no Tō ) , is located in the rear of Enies Lobby, behind the courthouse and across the drawbridge. It consists of a large tower, with the words "Enies Lobby" proudly displayed on it, and it is the tallest structure in Enies Lobby. It is known to have many floors, although how many is unclear, as only the floor of the director, Spandam , and some of the agents' rooms are shown. Generally, CP9 will just lounge around when not on missions. It is called the Tower of Law in the English versions.

This is the place where the fights between the Straw Hat Pirates and CP9 took place. It contains many different rooms and secret passages. During the Enies Lobby Arc , it took a lot of damage, first being sliced in half by Kaku 's Rankyaku "Amane Dachi." The top half of the tower continued to slide off whenever powerful attacks were being used, such as Luffy 's Gear 3 attack, until finally, during the fight between Luffy and Lucci , the Buster Call attack hit it and the structure collapsed. Other examples of the destruction of the tower are the holes in the walls caused by Franky and Fukurou's brawl and Nami's fight with Kalifa. Lots of interior damage is caused by Sanji's battle with Jabra, especially because of his Diable Jambe Flambage Shot . But the biggest damage was caused by Chopper's monster rampage. The tower is currently destroyed, along with the rest of Enies Lobby.

An underground tunnel leads from the basement of the Tower of Justice to the Bridge of Hesitation, allowing one to bypass the whirlpools. The entire passage is under the water.

The Bridge of Hesitation ( ためらいの橋 , Tamerai no Hashi ) is a large, two-sectioned drawbridge. The first section is a regular bridge that leads from the underground passage to the Gates of Justice, while the second is a drawbridge that goes through the gates.

The Bridge of Hesitation is the last stop for a criminal before the Gates of Justice . It was called the Bridge of Hesitation because once the prisoner is led here, they are so fearful of what will happen to them that they hesitate and try to run in the opposite direction but are stopped by the Marine guards. Once the criminal leaves this point, there is no going back for them, and their fate is sealed. They are then taken by ship to either Marine headquarters or Impel Down. These places give no chance of escape, and the prisoner usually dies at the location. Two years ago, a majority of the bridge was destroyed.

After the events of the Water 7 Saga , Enies Lobby was completely left in ruins. However, the members of CP9 managed to survive, thanks to Blueno 's Devil Fruit powers .
