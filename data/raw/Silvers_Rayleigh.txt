Silvers Rayleigh , also known as the "Dark King" , is a legendary retired pirate who served as the first mate of the former Roger Pirates , being known as the "Right Hand of the Pirate King" . He is one of the major figures from the time when Gol D. Roger , the "Pirate King", was still alive.

After the disbandment of the Roger Pirates and the execution of Roger, Rayleigh became a coating mechanic at the Sabaody Archipelago . Also, at some point after this, he became the husband of Shakuyaku and befriended Hatchan . Through Hatchan, Rayleigh became acquainted with the Straw Hat Pirates and volunteered to coat their ship so that they could continue their journey to the New World . Following the Summit War of Marineford , Rayleigh tutored Monkey D. Luffy in Haki to prepare him for the challenges ahead and two years later saw Luffy and his crew off from Sabaody Archipelago, completing the job he had started for them. After the Seven Warlords of the Sea were disbanded and the Emperor Blackbeard invaded Amazon Lily , Rayleigh personally intervened to put an end to the hostilities and intimidated the Emperor into retreating.

Rayleigh is an ally of the Straw Hat Pirates during the Sabaody Archipelago Arc and Return to Sabaody Arc , and served as Luffy's mentor during the two-year timeskip.

Rayleigh is an elderly man of relatively tall height with a very muscular build and tan skin. Two of his most distinguishing traits are his round glasses and facial hair, which is arranged embattled across his lower jaw. He also has a scar over his right eye, which he has had for over 44 years.

Rayleigh chooses to dress quite simply, usually just in a nearly plain t-shirt, shorts, and sandals. When out in public, he also seems to enjoy the cover of a large silver hooded cloak, though the hood is often down. Underneath these clothes, the old man's body is surprisingly built and very well-toned, a testament to his incredible strength.

When Rayleigh was younger, he had clean cut strawberry blond hair (black when first seen in the anime) and had a moderate amount of facial hair. Over the years, as his hair whitened, he grew it out. He also grew a beard from his initial striped facial hair. During Roger's last year alive, Rayleigh was noticeably taller, reaching almost the same height as his former captain. Regardless of his age, Rayleigh is a muscular man with a broad chest and well-built body.

During Rayleigh's first appearance in Buggy's flashback, he wore a t-shirt with an ornately decorated collar, a motif that appeared even in the long coat he wore during the Battle of Edd War . He wore an orange shirt, light-brown spotted Bermuda shorts, and simple sandals. He also has a silver-colored coat hanging from his shoulders.

After the two-year timeskip, Rayleigh now wears a black T-shirt as well as his silver hooded cloak.

Later after the Levely and his arrival on Amazon Lily , Rayleigh wore a yellow ochre top with sleeves and light-green pants with dark green zig-zag like diamond patterns.

Light-hearted, casual, fun-loving, and righteous, Rayleigh's attributes bear a striking resemblance to his former captain, Roger in the present day. When he served as first-mate, however, he was shown to have been more impatient, rational, and serious when compared to most other hands and sailors, easily irritated with the antics of the younger crew, and being more of a disciplinarian. His more serious demeanour also gave him some advantages, being the only one among his compatriots not to fall for Shakky's beauty and charm. However, he has mellowed since his crew disbanded, becoming much more relaxed even when training Luffy . While often casual, with a hint of seriousness and extreme strength hiding behind his glasses, Rayleigh enjoys going with the flow and appearing in unexpected places and in unexpected ways, especially given his status and age.

Rayleigh is not often headstrong, looking before he leaps and taking time to plan ahead before taking action, although he does not hesitate to act if need be. Even when entering into an unfortunate or unplanned situation, such as engaging a Marine Admiral in combat or having his ship sink in the Sea King -infested waters of the Grand Line , Rayleigh remains completely calm, pushing forward through adversity with no hesitation. However, Rayleigh has something of a fatherly side to him. This is shown when it comes to Luffy and Hancock, and when pushed, Rayleigh is capable of anger when provoked. When Blackbeard invaded Amazon Lily and tried to kill Hancock, Rayleigh stared furiously towards him, even referring to him as a brat whom he did not like.

He is typically open and talkative and, like Whitebeard , seems to enjoy talking about the past and its great times, as well as the good old days of their nearly bygone era. In spite of this, he appeared reluctant to get into the darker topic of the Void Century . He was willing to divulge the whole truth to Nico Robin if she wished, but she declined his offer. He is also wise and somewhat cautious, well aware of his own limitations and that he is far past his prime. He often comments on his age and how his body "does not work the way it used to", much like Garp , and Whitebeard. In spite of his legendary status, Rayleigh admitted he would be no match for Blackbeard in a straight-up fight at his age and was gambling on his reputation to scare him off.

As stated by Shakky , he loves alcohol and women, which he proved when he was first introduced, saving Camie from being mistreated by Disco while holding a hip flask. He prefers to be called "Ray-san". Despite once being enemies with the Whitebeard Pirates , Rayleigh shows great respect for their captain, like Roger, and shed a tear for Whitebeard's passing.

Rayleigh's relationship with most of the Roger Pirates is unknown. However, he had a close relationship with his captain, Gol D. Roger . He later became the first mate of the Oro Jackson , the ship of the man who would conquer the Grand Line and be crowned by the world as the Pirate King . Though he was close comrades with them, he tended to be exasperated with their antics, such as them attacking the Kuja Pirates to abduct Shakky, only to be easily defeated by them.

Rayleigh had a very close friendship with Roger, being his first mate and having known each other for many decades, to the point where he was called 'partner' by the Pirate King. After the man gave himself to the Marines , Rayleigh was unable to bring himself to directly witness the execution of his closest friend. He toasted a drink to the man with both tears and laughter as the blades passed and downed a glass as his first act in the Great Pirate Era .

Buggy and Shanks ' repeated bickering with each other often led to them being punished by Rayleigh. Despite that, Buggy and Shanks still have much respect for the former first mate of the crew.

Rayleigh was aware that Buggy was a pirate in the East Blue , which means he kept tabs on him. Twelve years ago, after Shanks saved Luffy from being eaten by the Lord of the Coast , Shanks reunited with Rayleigh at Sabaody Archipelago and told him about Luffy, which made Rayleigh curious to meet the boy.

He seems to appreciate Crocus for maintaining the Pirate King's health during their voyage in the New World . Despite the doctor's brief time with them, Rayleigh considers him a valued crewmate and expressed a desire to see him again.

Like the rest of the crew, he held Oden in high regard. Rayleigh wanted to help him make Wano Country open to the rest of the world, though Oden declined the offer.

Rayleigh has known Shakuyaku for over 44 years, as she was the Kuja Pirates ' First Mate , with his crew often squabbling with the Kuja in attempts to get her to join the Roger Pirates. He was the only one among the crew who did not make a fool of himself in her presence, which Shakuyaku took notice of, and she took a liking to him. Roughly 2 years after this encounter, Rayleigh became aware of Shakuyaku's feelings for him when he was given a love letter from her telling him to come and get her. However, Rayleigh abstained from pursuing a relationship, and while Roger found out and was angered, Rayleigh admitted that though he was interested, he merely did not want to be tied down.

40 years ago, when the crew went to Shakky's Rip-off Bar , Rayleigh chose to remain on the ship while the rest of the crew went to visit her. The following year after learning Shakuyaku was kidnapped, Rayleigh was notably distressed, but when he and the rest of the crew learned that she was captured as a slave on God Valley , Rayleigh and the rest of the crew passionately set out to rescue her. Upon rescuing her from Shepherd Sommers , Rayleigh embraced Shakuyaku and broke into tears, relieved that she was safe.

At some point after the disbandment of the Roger Pirates and Shakuyaku's retirement from pirate life, Rayleigh and Shakuyaku settled down together, becoming business partners and a married couple. When he had gone missing for the previous six months before her introduction, she was not worried because she knew what Rayleigh was capable of. Shakky and Rayleigh are in an open marriage, as she casually mentioned Rayleigh's relationships with other women without being bothered by it. Upon meeting Luffy, she informed him of the other major pirates that had arrived on the island around the same time as him. She then told them that they might find Rayleigh at Sabaody Park . After the Straw Hat Pirates returned from the Human Auctioning House , where they found Rayleigh, he and the entire Straw Hat crew gathered at Shakky's bar. Rayleigh trusts her implicitly, and he knows her qualities. With her deductions, Rayleigh managed to find Luffy after the war.

Hatchan is friends with Silvers Rayleigh after saving the pirate from a shipwreck as a child. Since then, the two were on good terms, but Rayleigh disapproved of Hatchan joining Fisher Tiger 's Sun Pirates .

After meeting them during the Celestial Dragon Hit-and-Run Incident , Rayleigh assisted the Straw Hat Pirates on several occasions. He coated the Thousand Sunny for free, and he also defended the crew from Kizaru . After the Straw Hats got back together two years after the Summit War of Marineford, he taught Nami the technicalities of sailing a coated ship. When the Marines pursued the crew, Rayleigh helped the Straw Hats escape.

Ever since Rayleigh had heard about Luffy from Shanks, he had wanted to meet him face-to-face. When Luffy found him on the Sabaody Archipelago , he was excited about hearing stories about Gol D. Roger. The more they talked, the more amused Rayleigh felt as he compared Luffy's demeanor to his old captain, just as Shanks had. He, like Shakky, has great expectations for him after Luffy stated his belief that the Pirate King has the most freedom on the sea. After the war at Marineford, he swam across the Calm Belt to reach Amazon Lily , where Luffy had been recuperating from his injuries and from the loss of Ace . From there, he helped Luffy break into Marineford with Jinbe in order for him to send the secret message to his crew. Rayleigh also mentored Luffy on how to master all three different forms of Haki over the course of a year and a half. Luffy strongly respected Rayleigh as a teacher, as he was even willing to endure hunger as punishment.

After the two-year timeskip, it is apparent that both of them became close from their training together. As he headed towards the Thousand Sunny , Luffy shouted to Rayleigh that he would become the Pirate King, leaving Rayleigh with a smile and tears in his eyes. Rayleigh himself later proudly proclaimed Luffy as his pupil to the Marines . When Luffy set sail, Rayleigh compared his late captain to Luffy, claiming that destiny is slowly but surely taking shape and that Luffy has become a man well fit to wear that Straw Hat .

Rayleigh also seems to have developed a friendship with the former Warlord of the Sea , Jinbe . At first, Jinbe could not believe his eyes when he saw Rayleigh in Amazon Lily. He took part in stealing a Marine ship, circling around Marineford, and the ringing of the Ox Bell.

Due to her hellish experience at the hands of the World Nobles , Hancock has a bitter hatred towards all men. However, Rayleigh is one of the few exceptions to this hatred because of his relationship with Shakuyaku and previously helping Hancock and her sisters return to Amazon Lily after their escape from Mary Geoise . While her sisters were shocked to see Rayleigh on Amazon Lily, Hancock remained single-minded about Luffy, only realizing later that Rayleigh was there. Hancock also permitted him to stay in Amazon Lily. Later, after parting ways with Jinbe, Hancock took Luffy and Rayleigh to Rusukaina Island for Luffy's special training. She said she would bring food for Luffy every day from Amazon Lily. When Rayleigh told her that no women would be allowed on the island during Luffy's training, she was furious but calmed down when Rayleigh told her that it was all for Luffy's own good. After the abolition of the Seven Warlords of the Sea , both he and Shakuyaku would go to Amazon Lily to help out the Kuja , where he would run into Blackbeard attacking the island and make a deal with him and the Marines for Hancock to reverse the effect of her Devil Fruit ability , and then everyone would leave afterwards.

Gloriosa is good friends with Rayleigh and Shakky , with the latter being her First Mate. In the past, the Kuja Pirates and the Roger Pirates clashed due to their attempts to get Shakky to become one of their crewmates. Roughly four years before the God Valley Incident , Gloriosa joined the Rocks Pirates , making them enemies on opposing crews. However, she and Rayleigh eventually became close friends, as he is one of the few people who refer to her by her birth name, Gloriosa. Before her return to Amazon Lily, Gloriosa lived with Rayleigh and Shakky on Sabaody Archipelago while sheltering the Gorgon Sisters after their escape from the World Nobles.

Rayleigh is an enemy of the World Government . He is one of the few people who know the true history and was willing to tell Robin about the Void Century , but she denied wanting to know further. The government considers such people a great threat.

In the past, Rayleigh and the other Roger Pirates encountered several Marines , especially Monkey D. Garp . The fact that he was a member of one of the strongest pirate crews made him a formidable enemy of the Marines. When Rayleigh fought Kizaru, he told him that if he were to get rid of his wanted poster, he could go into peaceful retirement. This means that Rayleigh still has an active bounty, though the amount has yet to be revealed. Kizaru replied that Rayleigh's bounty cannot simply be forgotten, as being a pirate, especially a member of the Roger Pirates, carries the crimes throughout one's entire life.

As a member of the Roger Pirates, Rayleigh had a rivalry with the Whitebeard Pirates . Nevertheless, as with his captain, Rayleigh has great respect for Edward Newgate and even shed a tear for the man's death at the pinnacle of the Summit War of Marineford .

Chinjao apparently holds a grudge against Rayleigh, as when Luffy reveals Rayleigh is the one who taught him to control Haki, Chinjao expressed displeasure to know the Pirate King's right hand still lives.

Even when Rayleigh first met Marshall D. Teach years ago back when he was still a member of the Whitebeard Pirates, while both the Roger and Whitebeard Pirates were rivals, despite Rayleigh having some respect for Whitebeard's crew, Teach was the only member on that crew whom Rayleigh despised. This hatred would grow, not only for Blackbeard's actions in killing his former captain but also for stealing Whitebeard's powers and claiming his territories for his own. Rayleigh continued to despise Blackbeard even more after the latter invaded Amazon Lily and tried to kill Boa Hancock. While Rayleigh was successful in scaring off the Emperor and foiling his plan, Rayleigh admitted he would not win if he had fought Blackbeard head on.

Due to Teach's original role as an apprentice pirate on the Whitebeard Pirates, Rayleigh mockingly called him "Whitebeard's Cabin Boy" when he confronted the latter at Amazon Lily.

Although the two have never met each other, Rayleigh considers Vegapunk a fool for revealing many secrets about the Void Century to the world, which he considers something that people of their age should not talk about, as it is something that people of the younger generations should figure out for themselves.

As the " Right Hand of the Pirate King ", Rayleigh is considered a living legend and one of the strongest pirates in history, said to be comparable to his late captain Roger and his crewmate Scopper Gaban in his prime. A man of worldwide renown, Rayleigh is famous among pirates and civilians alike, with Sanji even commenting about the widespread knowledge of his name. Rayleigh was strong enough to knock a Knight of God flying into a wall with one attack during the Incident of God Valley.

Despite his old age, Shakuyaku stated that he was "100 times stronger" than all of the Super Rookies by the time they first arrived on the Sabaody Archipelago . In his younger days, Rayleigh fought alongside his crew in battles against powerful adversaries like the Marines , Whitebeard Pirates , and Rocks Pirates , making him a true veteran and one of the most experienced pirates in the world. He also commanded the rest of Roger's crew as his first mate and held several duties, like supervising his subordinates, including the young apprentices Buggy and Shanks .

When Vice Admiral Garp was informed that Rayleigh may be on the Sabaody Archipelago, he told his men not to confront him, nor should Fleet Admiral Sengoku be informed. Garp reasoned that the Marines should not confront two legends, Whitebeard and Rayleigh, at the same time and even admitted that if the Marines were to make an honest attempt at capturing Rayleigh, they would need to do serious preparation if they did not want to lose countless soldiers in the attempt, a true testament to his extraordinary strength. His reputation alone was enough to have Blackbeard , one of the Four Emperors who had spent two years mastering the Yami Yami no Mi and the Gura Gura no Mi , retreat from Amazon Lily .

Rayleigh's immense battle prowess allowed him to fight on par with Admiral Kizaru , preventing him from attacking the Straw Hat Pirates. However, he himself has admitted that he is not as young as he used to be and, as a result, can tire in battle. He also stated that due to his old age, he would likely be incapable of defeating Blackbeard. This implies that when he was in the prime of his strength, he might have been able to beat Admiral Kizaru and pose a serious threat to Blackbeard. Kizaru himself acknowledged that the World Government would have to do more than just send him, a battalion of Marines, and a unit of Pacifistas so they could actually have a chance of capturing or killing the Dark King.

Rayleigh is also an expert in the coating business, as he performs such operations for various pirate ships heading down to Fish-Man Island , and he understands the workings of deep-sea currents.

Rayleigh has an extremely crafty and cunning mentality; he has managed to evade the grasp of the expansive World Government for over 20 years and quickly learned to exploit the slave-trading business on Sabaody Archipelago for personal gain (getting himself caught and sold to a wealthy bidder before escaping and robbing his "master"). Calm and level-headed at virtually any and all times, he was the individual to break up arguments and disputes aboard the Oro Jackson as first mate and the right-hand man to Gol D. Roger himself.

Rayleigh knows the "true history" of the world from his time with Roger. However, he has also stated that, as a pirate crew lacking the archaeological knowledge of scholars of Ohara 's caliber, they could have interpreted that information differently than the Ohara scholars. However, this is questionable given the Roger Pirates had Kouzuki Oden, who possessed the knowledge on how to read and write the language on the Poneglyphs.

Another noticeable trait of Rayleigh is his incredible perceptive and deductive capability. This is shown when he was (within a matter of moments) easily able to deduce what had happened after ending up on the slave auction stage on the Sabaody Archipelago.

Rayleigh is also good at intimidation and false threats; by using his legendary reputation, he could intimidate both the Marines and Blackbeard Pirates into obediently leaving Amazon Lily, including Blackbeard himself, who had grown more powerful than Rayleigh, into giving up on stealing Boa Hancock's Devil Fruit.

Rayleigh is a very capable teacher; in a year and a half, he taught Luffy all the basics of Haki and how to better utilize his Devil Fruit, allowing Luffy to use Gear 2 and Gear 3 without their side effects and create Gear 4 . Luffy would also remember Rayleigh's lessons to help him master advanced levels of Haki. This would all greatly contribute to Luffy's rise as one of the Four Emperors a few months after completing his training under Rayleigh.

Rayleigh possesses immense physical strength despite his elderly age. He was capable of killing several Sea Kings with his bare hands even after swimming to Amazon Lily from outside the Calm Belt . In the anime, he could throw bullets with extreme force and accuracy to destroy cannonballs fired at him.

Rayleigh is extremely fast and possesses extraordinary reflexes. When removing Camie's exploding collar, he patiently calculated the time when the collar released itself and quickly tossed it away before it detonated. He could also intercept Kizaru's attack when the admiral was about to kill Roronoa Zoro , and also managed to keep up with his light-enhanced speed.

Rayleigh is a tremendously skilled and powerful swordsman with decades of combat experience since his time with the Roger Pirates. He was able to fight on equal grounds with Admiral Kizaru, who himself is a highly skilled swordsman when wielding his Ama no Murakumo , and managed to cut and draw blood from the admiral's face by combining Armament Haki with his swordsmanship. This is even more impressive given that Rayleigh has not used his sword in quite a while due to being retired. He can also create powerful compressed air slashes, as shown when he cut the ground to prevent Marines from chasing after Luffy.

Rayleigh is one of the very few people in the world who can use all three types of Haki. He is also a capable teacher, as he taught Luffy about each type of Haki and trained him in the basics.

Rayleigh possesses immense mastery in the usage of Supreme King Haki , a rare form of Haki that only one in several million people in the world can use. His level of skill is enough to render a specific target or entire crowds completely unconscious, as shown when he knocked out Rosward Shalria before disabling an entire hall room full of hostiles. Rayleigh's Supreme King Haki was at least twice as potent as Luffy's by the time of the Fish-Man Island Arc ; Rayleigh could have knocked out all 100,000 of the New Fish-Man Pirates opposing the Straw Hats, whereas Luffy overwhelmed only half that. Most notably, he is among the small "handful" of users who are capable of using the advanced application of this type of Haki, able to "infuse" his body or objects with Supreme King Haki to increase his attack power, signified by thin streams of black lightning emanating from his body. In the anime, he visibly infuses his sword with this Haki along with his body with notable lightning that incapacitates several Blackbeard Pirates in the area.

Rayleigh is a master of Armament Haki , which allowed him to kick and redirect an attack from Kizaru, and hurt Luffy with a simple flick. He is able to imbue his Haki into certain parts of his body or objects to harden them and emit his Haki, an advanced application, to repel and injure opponents without touching them directly. He is also able to use a high-grade version of this technique, in which he channels his Haki into his opponent or objects and destroys his opponent or objects from the inside out.

In the anime, when Rayleigh uses advanced Armament Haki, it is shown as a red-colored flowing aura. In the anime, he was able to block a kick from Marco in his hybrid form with a single Armament-coated finger.

Rayleigh is a master of Observation Haki . He could sense that there were five hundred creatures on Rusukaina who were stronger than Luffy before his training had begun and used it to dodge an elephant's attack with his eyes closed. When he arrived at Amazon Lily, Rayleigh was able to sense that Luffy was on the island. He also has knowledge of the advanced application of this type of Haki and how high-level users can see into the future.

Rayleigh possesses a double-edged straight sword, with a cross hilt and a large round pommel, roughly half as long as his body height. When he defended Luffy from the Marines, he used a katana, which he keeps in a sheath.

During his youth, Rayleigh stole a ship due to his house burning down and needing a place to live. Sometime after that, he met Gol D. Roger , who was impressed by the ship. Roger said that it was a fated meeting and asked Rayleigh to join him. Rayleigh simply laughed at his invitation and declined his offer. Despite his initial refusal, Rayleigh later engaged in piracy and became first mate of the Roger Pirates .

Around 44 years ago, the Roger Pirates attacked the Kuja Pirates , with the goal of having Shakuyaku join them. Unfortunately, everyone, save for Rayleigh, was too lovestruck by her to actually fight, leading to her easily subduing nearly the entire crew. Rayleigh was left annoyed as Shakky had her subordinates steal their treasure, although his composure was broken by a bit of her flirting toward him. While the Kuja attempted to make off with the treasure, Gaban spotted Garp's ship in the distance, forcing the Kuja to flee from the incoming threat. While they did so, Roger rallied his men in preparation for a fight.

42 years ago, sometime following one of their usual "battles" with the Kuja Pirates, Rayleigh met privately with Roger to discuss Shakky, who had just quit her crew. Roger mentioned that Shakky had seemed sick , to which Rayleigh revealed that he had received a love letter from her telling him to come and get her. Roger was enraged by Rayleigh's lack of interest in Shakky, forcing Rayleigh to try to justify it as simply not wanting to be tied down. Unfortunately, it was not enough, and the whole interaction ended up putting Roger into a sulk.

Sometime after, news reached the crew that Shakky had settled down on Hachinosu . With encouragement from his crew, Roger snapped out of his bad mood and declared that they would sail to find her. Come 40 years ago, they did. Under the excuse of not feeling well, Rayleigh remained on the ship and sang " Binks' Sake ", while the rest of the crew went to visit Shakky. His lack of a presence ended up greatly disappointing her and caused her to faint. A year later, Shakky was abducted from Hachinosu, the news of which distressed Rayleigh and the rest of the crew.

38 years ago, after learning that she was to be given away as a slave on God Valley , Rayleigh and the rest of the crew promptly set out to rescue her. The Roger Pirates clashed with the Rocks Pirates and the Knights of God , taking part in what would later be known as the God Valley Incident . During said incident, Rayleigh was given the go-ahead by Roger to be the one to rescue Shakky. His timing proved ideal, as Shepherd Sommers , one of the Knights of God, was preparing to kill her at that moment. Rayleigh quickly attacked Sommers and pushed him into a wall before he could do so, successfully saving Shakky's life. Afterward, Rayleigh apologized for not having sought her out sooner, and the two tearfully embraced. Before they could share intimate words, however, Roger and Gaban reminded them of the precarious situation outside, forcing them to flee. As they fled, Rayleigh attempted to tell her something, but Shakky hushed him, having already known what it was. After dropping Shakky off at their ship, Rayleigh and Gaban rushed back inland to assist Roger and Garp.

By the time they arrived, the fight was already over, with all three of the combatants downed. While the Knights of God finished off Rocks, Rayleigh and Gaban made off with Roger and Garp, successfully rescuing the pair. After dropping off Garp with the Marines and escaping the collapsing island, the Roger Pirates successfully got away with all of their objectives completed on God Valley. Sometime later, while spending time with Shakky, Roger became well enough to finally open the treasure they secured. However, to everyone's shock, a baby Shanks was present in one of the treasure chests.

28 years before the current storyline, Roger fell ill from an incurable disease, leading the crew to take one final journey together and venture into the Grand Line together. At some point, Rayleigh scolded Buggy and Shanks for constantly arguing over whether the North Pole or South Pole was colder and shortly after joined the rest of the crew in a battle against an unnamed enemy ship.

In the New World at Edd War , Buggy panicked at the potential conflict between two infamous pirate crews: the Roger Pirates and the Golden Lion Pirates . Rayleigh was present, and he held Buggy back, telling him not to worry. When Roger rejected Shiki 's offer to become his right-hand man, Rayleigh prepared for battle along with the other Roger Pirates. A battle between the two crews ensued, and a change in the weather allowed the Roger Pirates to sink half of Shiki's ships.

At some point 26 years ago, the Roger Pirates were causing a ruckus on an island. After defeating a group of Marines , they learned that the Whitebeard Pirates docked on the other side of the island. The Roger Pirates were then confronted by Kouzuki Oden . Rayleigh and Scopper Gaban prepared to fight him, but Roger charged in and blew Oden away with a single slash. The Roger Pirates clashed with the Whitebeard Pirates for three days. After the battle, the crews formed a truce and exchanged gifts. The Roger Pirates were later joined by Oden and his family as well as Inuarashi and Nekomamushi . At some point, the Roger Pirates visited Skypiea .

After leaving Skypiea, the crew's voyage took them to Water 7 , one of the Wolf bridges , Sabaody Archipelago , and Fish-Man Island . Sometime later, they left Kouzuki Toki , Momonosuke , Hiyori , Inuarashi, and Nekomamushi at Wano Country . Sometime after the visit to Zou , the Roger Pirates finally reached Laugh Tale .

A year before his death, Roger disbanded the crew in secret. Roger was the first to leave, and the crew tearfully parted ways with him. They later returned Oden to Wano Country, and they bid farewell to him as well.

Before he handed himself over to the Marines, Roger told Rayleigh that he would not die. Unwilling to watch the execution of his closest friend, Rayleigh toasted a drink to the man with a laugh as the blades passed and downed a glass as his first act in the Great Pirate Era. Rayleigh stated that he laughed (and cried and drank) nonetheless upon hearing the message that Roger had left the world.

Sometime later, Rayleigh was saved by a young Hatchan from drowning in a shipwreck. Since then, Rayleigh has been on good terms with Hachi. He, along with Shakuyaku and Nyon , also helped protect the Gorgon Sisters after their escape from slavery from the World Nobles . Eventually, Rayleigh became a coating mechanic on Sabaody Archipelago and, at some point, married Shakuyaku.

When news of Shiki's escape reached Rayleigh, he remarked on it and wondered what Shiki was up to.

12 years prior to the series, Rayleigh met his former apprentice Shanks again and noticed the latter lost his arm and his hat. Rayleigh asked him what occurred as he told him of a boy that he met in the East Blue who had the same passion as his former captain. Intrigued, Rayleigh desired to meet this boy someday.

He had been missing for six months prior to Luffy's arrival on the archipelago.

The Spade Pirates came across Rayleigh when they needed to have their ship coated in order to reach Fish-Man Island, with the Masked Deuce and him hitting it off. Rayleigh then coated their ship, with none of the Spade Pirates, except for Skull realizing who he was.

Rayleigh let himself be captured to be sold as a slave, and when the mermaid Camie was brought into the Human Auctioning House , Rayleigh discreetly used Supreme King Haki to knock out Disco , although his fellow slave Stansen recognized what he did. As an auction took place, Rayleigh broke out of his cell and stole the money that people had bid for their slaves before making his way to the auction stage. There, he saw Hatchan and ascertained that he and his allies, the Straw Hat Pirates , had attacked a World Noble to save Camie. Rayleigh then unleashed Supreme King Haki, knocking out most of the people in the auction hall, and he recognized Monkey D. Luffy , saying he had wanted to meet the Straw Hat captain.

Rayleigh then grabbed Camie's exploding collar and ripped it off, tossing it aside before it could explode. He surveyed the room and saw that the Kid Pirates and Heart Pirates , along with the Straw Hats, had survived his Haki blast, and he then turned his attention to Hatchan, thanking the Straw Hats for protecting him. The pirates then headed out of the auction hall, and Rayleigh told the Straw Hats to meet him at Grove 13 later. After the three pirate crews had fought through the Marines laying siege to the auction house, Rayleigh was able to run off with Hatchan.

He then revealed to the Straw Hats what had really happened to Roger, and they talked about Crocus and Shanks . Nico Robin then asked Rayleigh about the Void Century and Roger's writing on the Poneglyph in Skypiea but opted to continue researching for herself after Rayleigh warned about how she might use the information. Usopp then asked if the One Piece existed, but was stopped by Luffy, who wanted to discover the One Piece for himself. Rayleigh asked Luffy if he really intended to conquer the New World, as Luffy states he will become the freest man on the sea if he is Pirate King. Rayleigh was impressed by his ideology, seeing the similarities to Roger like Shanks saw. Rayleigh then went to coat the Straw Hats' ship , not charging them anything in return for them helping Hatchan, and gave them his Vivre Card , telling them to find him in three days.

A short time later, Marine Admiral Kizaru came and attacked the Straw Hats, but before he could kill Roronoa Zoro , Rayleigh came in and intercepted him. Rayleigh tried to see if Kizaru would leave the Straw Hats alone, but Kizaru would not abandon his mission. The Straw Hats immediately started running away, and Kizaru attempted to go after them, but Rayleigh stopped him with a sword. As Rayleigh sparred with Kizaru, he witnessed Warlord of the Sea Bartholomew Kuma joining some Pacifistas who were attacking the Straw Hats and saw as Kuma made the Straw Hats disappear. Kuma then went to Rayleigh and revealed that he was an officer of the Revolutionary Army and was acting to help the Straw Hats escape. Rayleigh watched as Kuma then transported the remaining Straw Hats off the island.

While Whitebeard's death was aired at Sabaody, Rayleigh, who had escaped Kizaru's arrest, watched from afar while drinking. He remarked that Whitebeard was finally dead with a smile on his face and tears in his eyes.

After the Summit War of Marineford , Rayleigh set off on a ship to Amazon Lily . However, before he could even reach the Calm Belt , his ship sank in a storm and thus forced him to swim the rest of the way to the island. He killed several Sea Kings when he was getting closer to the shore of the island, something which surprised and almost frightened the Heart Pirates who were watching from the shore. When he got to the shore, he remembered Trafalgar Law from the Sabaody Archipelago and told them he thought Luffy was on the island.

He saw Luffy and Jinbe coming toward him. When Luffy asked about his crew, Rayleigh said he doubted they were all back yet. After telling Luffy that Law said to take it easy for another two weeks, he proceeded to greet Nyon, Boa Hancock, and her sisters, whom he had met 13 years ago; he had protected them when they had escaped from the Celestial Dragons. Elder Nyon is an old friend of his, and Rayleigh passed on a message from Shakky. He reassured them that the Marines would not even think to look for Luffy on Amazon Lily and that he was completely safe there.

Rayleigh recalled what Kuma whispered to him back on the archipelago. He explained that Kuma told him about the general direction in which he sent Luffy, which happened to lead right to Amazon Lily. After going back through the events of the war with Shakky, she was able to surmise (correctly on all three accounts) that Boa Hancock was hiding Luffy on her home island, that she was doing so out of love for Luffy, and also as a means of hiding him from the Marines. Rayleigh commented that a woman's intuition was a truly frightening thing. He said he would get to the main reason he came to find Luffy. Rayleigh asked him if he wanted to return to Sabaody, and Luffy replied yes. Rayleigh reminded him of what happened there before, saying that gathering his crew there again would lead to the same result as before. He started to propose an idea he has to Luffy, giving him the choice of rejecting it if he so chooses.

He infiltrated Marineford with Luffy and Jinbe and took part in stealing a Marine ship and circling Marineford once with it. He was on the Perfume Yuda , with two Kuja Pirates pulling on his cheek. He told Luffy that his crew most likely got the meaning of his message, due to its abnormal nature, and said that he had a hunch that the others would understand the message.

He and Luffy went to Rusukaina for special training. Rayleigh told Hancock that none of the Kujas are allowed on the island during the training and that if she was worried, then she should pray for Luffy. Once the Kuja Pirates left, Rayleigh began Luffy's training by mentioning that the island was once inhabited, but the chaotic climate changes and the ferocious nature of the wildlife eventually killed all the natives. As a wild elephant came charging at them, Rayleigh explained the basic concepts of Haki, using the elephant as a demonstration for them. Rayleigh told Luffy that there are three kinds of Haki: Observation Haki, Armament Haki, and Supreme King Haki. He used Observation Haki to dodge the elephant's attack and used Armament Haki to repel the elephant. When Luffy started to understand Haki, Rayleigh subdued the elephant using the Supreme King Haki.

The two began training on the island. Luffy realized that he had seen these techniques before in some form or another during his adventures. Rayleigh hoped to use those two years to teach him the basics of controlling the different types of Haki, as it would take longer with a normal person.

During Luffy's training, he developed Gear 4 . Luffy asked Rayleigh on how to improve its attack power, and Rayleigh warned him about the consequences of using this technique.

While teaching Observation Haki to Luffy, he challenged Luffy to dodge 100 times while blindfolded or he would not be allowed to eat. When Luffy was attacking blindly, he accidentally hit Rayleigh's dinner, causing him to hit Luffy out of anger and annoyance as Luffy sensed his emotions. During a later conversation, Rayleigh remarked on how Luffy was proficient in sensing the feelings of living things. He also mentioned that there are people who can see a glimpse into the future with Observation Haki and asked Luffy what would he do if he met one of these people.

After teaching Luffy the basics of Haki, Rayleigh informed him that Haki can grow stronger when facing stronger foes.

Rayleigh returned to Sabaody Archipelago half a year before Luffy. In Shakky's bar, he commented on how strong the Straw Hat Pirates had become. He also finished coating the Thousand Sunny.

Rayleigh met Nami, Robin, Franky, Chopper, and Usopp on the Thousand Sunny. He informed them that Luffy had arrived on the island and is about to teach Nami on how to operate a coated ship. He told her to move the ship to Grove 42 and to wait until everyone assembled, saying it was finally time to restart. Rayleigh went to Grove 46 to check up on Luffy. He also commented on how Luffy strengthened his Haki.

After Luffy had declared that he would be the Pirate King, Rayleigh smiled with tears in his eyes. As the Marines begin their pursuit, Rayleigh drew a line on the ground with his sword and warned the Marines not to cross it, declaring that Luffy is his student. By doing so, he bought some extra time for the Straw Hat Pirates to escape to Fish-Man Island.

Once the Straw Hats left, he revealed in a conversation with Shakky that a previous owner of Luffy's straw hat was in fact, Roger. After reminiscing about the day, he first met Roger, Rayleigh started tearing up and commented that Luffy is a man worthy of that hat. Rayleigh added that it is not a bad idea to live a little longer.

Rayleigh was gambling while reading the news of the Straw Hats' new bounties after the events at Dressrosa.

After the World Government abolished the Seven Warlords of the Sea , Rayleigh and Shakuyaku sailed to Amazon Lily , which was already under attack by the Marines and the Blackbeard Pirates . Rayleigh threatened Teach who was choking Hancock and intimidated him into letting her go.

Weeks after the attack on Amazon Lily was over, Rayleigh sat with the current and former leaders of the island and discussed the situation and the Marines' new weapons, the Seraphim .

Sometime later, during the Egghead Incident , Rayleigh got drunk at Shakky's bar while listening to Vegapunk 's world-wide message about the Void Century . When Vegapunk declared that the Roger Pirates had already discovered the full truth decades earlier, Rayleigh chastised Vegapunk for not letting the younger generation figure it out themselves. As the broadcast neared its end, Rayleigh passed out drunk at the bar, so Shakky draped a blanket over him.

In the manga, when a younger Rayleigh's glasses were briefly seen, they had a circular shape. In the anime initially, they had an oblong shape. In the same flashback in the anime, his scar is missing, presumably because the scar is difficult to see in the manga. Also, Rayleigh's hair was black instead of the strawberry blond seen in later flashbacks. This changing of a character's design to a more generic one occurs several times in the series, notably with Marco .

Rayleigh mentioning his second suggestion to Luffy was not in the manga. In the manga, Rayleigh is the only one who fights the elephant. It was never shown in the manga how Luffy, Jinbe and Rayleigh got back into Marineford. In the anime, Luffy's actions in Marineford are seen, though in the manga they were only explained later.

In the final battle between the Roger Pirates and the Whitebeard Pirates, Rayleigh was shown briefly clashing with Marco, using his advanced Armament to stop the latter's attack with a single finger. In the manga, the battle as a whole was little elaborated upon.
