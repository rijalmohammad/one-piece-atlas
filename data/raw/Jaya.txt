Jaya is a lawless island in Paradise popular with pirates . 400 years ago, it—with more than twice its current landmass—was the homeland of the Shandia .

Canonically, it is the fifth place visited by the Straw Hat Pirates on the Grand Line and the main setting of the Jaya Arc .

Jaya consists mostly of tropical rainforests inhabited by gigantic insects and the elusive South Birds. The island itself has the shape of a jagged "U".

In truth, Jaya's landmass was physically halved some 400 years ago. Originally, Jaya was in the shape of a skull, with current Jaya featuring just the skull's "jaws". Due to over half of it being launched into the White-White Sea by the Knock Up Stream (which was possible due to seismic activity shattering the island's crust), only the part that made up the skull's mouth remains. The Shandia civilization once inhabited Jaya, but them and their legendary city were shot up in the sky alongside Upper Yard.

The Knock Up Stream is a periodic event known to happen near Jaya, consisting a huge eruption of water that reaches high into the sky. Lining this eruption with the Imperial Cumulus , the cloud where Skypiea is located, allows one to reach the Sky Island from the ocean. This method of travel is considered the hardest and most dangerous form to reach Skypiea. Moreover, the Saruyama Alliance had established a few territories around Jaya, where they once conducted underwater explorations in search of ruins, artifacts or anything else that could prove Shandora's existence.

Mock Town is the only urban area in the whole of Jaya. Despite its resort-like appearance, it is considered a lawless town where all sorts pirates gather, away from the Marines or the World Government 's reach.

Mouth Bay ( マウスベイ , Mausu Bei ) is the part of the ocean that connects the two extremities of Jaya Island.

It is considered the territory of the Shoujou Pirates , led by Shoujou of the Saruyama Alliance . The Straw Hat Pirates sailed through this bay in order to find Mont Blanc Cricket .

South Grave is the rainforest located to the southeast of Jaya. It is inhabited by gigantic insects and South Birds, who are able to control the former in order to protect the forest from invaders.

The Straw Hats were sent here by Cricket in order to capture a South Bird so they could use it as an ordinary compass within the Grand Line, allowing them to locate the Knock Up Stream the next day.

Mont Blanc Cricket once lived in a small brick residence on Jaya's eastern coast. This house, however, was only half of an entire one, due to the other half being shot up in the sky alongside the rest of Upper Yard. As such, Cricket covered the missing half with a plywood façade of a huge castle, thus creating an illusion that could deceive onlookers coming from the ocean.

The house served as the headquarters of the Saruyama Alliance while they operated on Jaya.

Upper Yard is the missing half of Jaya that got shot up into Skypiea around 400 years ago, thus it is no longer part of Jaya.

Many centuries ago, Jaya's native Shandia were entrusted with a Poneglyph and related writings from the Void Century . These became the centerpiece of their "golden city" Shandora , and were viciously guarded by generation after generation, permitting no outsiders on the island.

Some 400 years ago, the Lvneel explorer Mont Blanc Noland made contact with Jaya, and after many trials managed to win the trust and friendship of then-chief Kalgara . Though Noland brought enthusiastic reports of the Shandia and their treasures back to Lvneel and planned a return voyage, the Knock-Up Stream launched half of Jaya—including Shandora—into Skypiea during his absence. While the Shandia struggled to adjust to their new environment and wars from the land-hungry Skypieans, their vaunted city was dismissed as a myth by the world at large (with Noland even being executed as a fraud).

In present day, Jaya appears to lie entirely outside the World Government 's attention, with no Marine presence and Mock Town the only settlement of note. Due to the lack of authorities, the island is a major pirate center.
